Your estimated price can depend on the total moving distance and the household items selected for transportation.

Yes. RelocateSeva is designed to provide transportation tracking so customers can monitor their moving journey.

RelocateSeva includes appliance and technology setup support for items such as TVs, refrigerators, washing machines and other household equipment.

Can I choose my relocation location? +

Yes. During the relocation process, you can provide your moving location and destination details required for your service.
